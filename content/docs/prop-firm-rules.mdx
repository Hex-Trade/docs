Most prop firms care about **rules**, not whether the trade came from an algorithm. Hextrade will place what you enable. The firm will still breach you.

## Rules that show up most often

| Rule | What it usually means |
| --- | --- |
| **Daily loss** | Stop trading (or fail) when today’s realized + unrealized hit a dollar cap. |
| **Max / trailing drawdown** | Equity cannot fall more than X from a high-water mark. |
| **Consistency** | One huge day cannot be most of the profit target. |
| **News lockout** | No open risk through CPI, FOMC, NFP, and similar. |
| **Overnight / weekend** | Flat before the cash close or before Friday halt. |
| **Restricted products** | Some firms ban CL, crypto, or mixing minis and micros. |

## What to use on Hextrade

- Prefer algorithms tagged **Prop Firm Ready** on [Algorithms](/algorithms).
- Avoid **Overnight** / **Non-Prop** families on funded accounts unless the firm allows holds.
- Size in **micros** and keep copy-trade multipliers at or below `1.0` until you know the daily-loss math.
- Watch the [Calendar](/calendar) on news days.

<Warning>
  A 2.0× copy-trade multiplier on a funded follower can violate daily-loss even when the master is fine. Tailor each follower.
</Warning>
