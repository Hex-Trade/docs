Futures / order-flow. Algorithms, webhooks, and copy trade. Webhook `platformType`: `volumetrica`.

## How to connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Add account">
    [Accounts](/accounts) → **Add account** → Volumetrica. Premium is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Username and password">
    The login from your Volumetrica or introducing broker. Verify and add the account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to sign contracts

Sign the Volumetrica / FCM account agreement and market-data addendums in their portal before you expect live fills. Hextrade only stores the connection.
