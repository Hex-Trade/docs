Prop-firm futures. Algorithms, webhooks, and copy trade. Webhook `platformType`: `projectx`.

## How to connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Add account">
    [Accounts](/accounts) → **Add account** → ProjectX. Premium is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the firm server">
    Example: `tradeify.projectx.com`, `topstepx.com`, `lucidtrading.projectx.com`. The list is the firms Hextrade supports.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Username and password">
    The login from that firm’s ProjectX / TopstepX dashboard — not your Hextrade email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify, then select accounts">
    Check the evaluation or funded accounts you want and add them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Servers include Aqua, Alpha Futures, Blue Guardian, Bulenox, E8, Lucid, TickTickTrader, Topstep, Tradeify, TX3, and others. Full picker is in Add account.

## How to sign contracts

1. Buy or activate the evaluation on the **firm website**.
2. Sign that firm’s trader agreement and payout rules there.
3. Wait until the firm emails ProjectX credentials.
4. Then connect in Hextrade. Hextrade does not sign the prop contract for you.
