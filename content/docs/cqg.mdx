Futures and derivatives. Algorithms yes. Webhooks and copy trade are not available. Webhook `platformType`: `cqg`.

## How to connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Add account">
    [Accounts](/accounts) → **Add account** → CQG. Premium is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Demo or Live">
    Match the CQG environment your FCM issued.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Username and password">
    Verify, select the account, and add it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to sign contracts

CQG access is issued by an FCM or broker. Sign their account paperwork and exchange market-data agreements in the CQG / FCM portal first.
