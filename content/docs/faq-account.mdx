---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I create an account?">
    Register at [dash.hextrade.io/signup](https://dash.hextrade.io/signup) with email or Google. Walkthrough: [Quick start](/quick-start).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I log in?">
    [dash.hextrade.io/login](https://dash.hextrade.io/login). After sign-in you land on **Accounts**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need Premium to start?">
    Free accounts are **view only**. You can browse algorithms, portfolios, and analytics. You cannot connect brokers, run algorithms, copy trade, or deploy. Premium is required to do anything live. See [Subscriptions](/subscriptions).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can’t I add another broker account?">
    Your plan sets an **account limit**. The Accounts page shows how many you have used. Upgrade on [Subscriptions](/subscriptions) if you hit the cap.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I change billing or cancel?">
    Open **Subscriptions** in the sidebar, or **Settings → Manage billing**. After a plan change, new limits and algorithm access apply as shown on that page.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
