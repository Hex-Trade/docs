Prop forex and CFDs. Algorithms and webhooks. No copy trade. Webhook `platformType`: `matchtrader`.

## How to connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Add account">
    [Accounts](/accounts) → **Add account** → MatchTrader. Premium is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Username, password, server">
    Server URL comes from the prop firm’s MatchTrader email. Verify and add.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to sign contracts

Sign the firm’s evaluation or funded agreement on their site. MatchTrader access is issued after that.
