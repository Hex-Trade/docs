---
title: Calendar
description: Macro events that can move markets — importance, forecast, and actuals.
---

Open **Economic calendar** in the sidebar. Use it to plan risk around high-impact prints, not as a signal service.

## What you’ll see

- Date, time, country / region, and event name
- Importance — high, medium, or low
- Actual vs forecast on past events when available

## How to use it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the calendar">
    Sidebar → **Economic calendar**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan high-impact rows">
    Central bank decisions, employment, and GDP usually matter more than low-importance prints.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Size around the window">
    Many traders avoid opening large positions right before a high-impact release.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  This is the macro event calendar. Day-by-day P&L for a strategy mix is the **Calendar** tab in [Portfolio](/portfolio).
</Note>
