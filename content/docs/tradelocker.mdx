Multi-asset / prop CFDs. Algorithms and webhooks. No copy trade. Webhook `platformType`: `tradelocker`.

## How to connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Add account">
    [Accounts](/accounts) → **Add account** → TradeLocker. Premium is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Demo or Live">
    Match the environment on your TradeLocker login.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Email, password, server name">
    Server is the firm’s TradeLocker brand (example: `PLEXY`). Verify, then select accounts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to sign contracts

Sign the prop or broker agreement on the firm’s TradeLocker dashboard first. Hextrade only uses those credentials.
