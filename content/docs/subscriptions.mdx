Open **Subscriptions** in the sidebar or go to [dash.hextrade.io/subscriptions](https://dash.hextrade.io/subscriptions).

<Video src="/videos/Purchase-Sub.mp4" />

## What you’ll see

- Active plans and status (active, expired)
- Which algorithms and features each plan unlocks
- Account limits and copy-trade group limits

## How access works

- Some algorithms require a paid plan. The Algos page sends you here if you do not have access.
- Account slots on [Accounts](/accounts) follow the plan.
- Copy-trade group count can also be capped.
- **Portfolio deploy** requires Premium.
- TradingView username in Settings (exclusive indicators) is Premium and Ultimate.

## Change plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Subscriptions">
    Review current plan(s) and what they include.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upgrade or subscribe">
    Use the upgrade actions on the page, or **Manage billing** in [Settings](/settings).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm limits">
    New algorithm access and account caps apply after the plan updates.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Special “unlimited” plans hide some caps. The Accounts page always shows the live limit.
