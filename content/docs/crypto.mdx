Hyperliquid is **perpetual futures**, not CME contracts and not CFDs. You trade a **size** in the coin. Collateral and P&L are in USDC.

- Native crypto — `BTC`, `ETH`, `SOL`
- Tokenized equity indexes on the HIP-3 `xyz` dex — `XYZ100`, `SP500`

Exchange futures sizing is on [Futures](/futures). CFD lots and pt/lot are on [CFDs](/cfds).

## Tokenized indexes

**XYZ100** tracks Nasdaq 100. **SP500** tracks S&P 500. They are HIP-3 perps on Hyperliquid (`xyz:XYZ100`, `xyz:SP500`), not `NQ` / `ES` and not `NAS100` / `US500` CFDs.

Hextrade maps the usual algo and webhook tickers onto those coins so the same payload can hit Hyperliquid:

`NAS100` on a CFD broker stays a CFD lot. `NAS100` on Hyperliquid becomes `xyz:XYZ100`.

<Warning>

</Warning>

## Crypto perps

`BTC`, `ETH`, and `SOL` are native Hyperliquid perps. Webhook aliases such as `BTCUSD` and `ETHUSDT` map to `BTC` and `ETH`.

These are not CME Bitcoin / Ether futures (`BTC`, `ETH` on CME) and not `BTCUSD` CFDs.

## Size and P&L

```
P&L (USDC) = price move × size
```

Size is the Hyperliquid `sz` (coin units), not CME contracts and not MT5 lots. Min size follows each market’s `szDecimals`.

Tokenized indexes quote like the cash index. Crypto perps quote in USDC per coin.

## What to set on a Hyperliquid account

Connect the wallet and agent key from [Accounts](/accounts). `platformType` is `hyperliquid`. Send `XYZ100`, `SP500`, `NAS100`, `US500`, `BTC`, or `ETH` — Hextrade canonicalizes to the coin in the table.

Confirm one small fill before you scale.
