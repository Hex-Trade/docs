Four steps to get onto the dashboard. Each step has a walkthrough video.

## 1. Create your account

Register at [dash.hextrade.io/signup](https://dash.hextrade.io/signup) with email or Google.

<Video src="/videos/Create-Account.mp4" />

## 2. Log in

Sign in at [dash.hextrade.io/login](https://dash.hextrade.io/login). After login you land on **Accounts**.

<Video src="/videos/Login-Account.mp4" />

<Tip>
  Enable 2FA in [Settings](/settings) before you connect funded accounts.
</Tip>

## 3. Purchase premium (optional)

Free accounts can connect brokers and explore the dashboard. Premium unlocks paid algorithms, portfolio deploy, and higher limits.

Purchase at [dash.hextrade.io/subscriptions](https://dash.hextrade.io/subscriptions).

<Video src="/videos/Purchase-Sub.mp4" />

Need the plan details? See [Subscriptions](/subscriptions).

## 4. Connect a broker

Open **Accounts** → **Add account** and pick your platform. The full list, copy-trade coverage, and webhook `platformType` values are on [Brokers](/brokers).

<Video src="/videos/Add-Account.webm" />
