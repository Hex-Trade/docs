## What you can do

- **Connect brokers** — ProjectX, Tradovate, NinjaTrader, Rithmic, TradeLocker, and more on [Accounts](/accounts).

<AllPlatformPills />
- **Run algorithms** — Browse families like Raptor and Blaze, then attach a connected account.
- **Copy trade** — One master account, many followers, each with its own risk multiplier.
- **Build a portfolio** — Mix algorithms, review the seven analysis tabs, then deploy.
- **Trade manually** — Full-screen chart and order ticket on [Trading](/trading).
- **Use TradingView** — Send alerts to Hextrade with a webhook payload.
