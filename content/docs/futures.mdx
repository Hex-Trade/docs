A futures contract is a standardized agreement to buy or sell an instrument at a future date. Size, tick, and expiration are set by the exchange (CME, COMEX, NYMEX, CBOT).

- Traded on an exchange through a futures broker
- Margin and daily settlement follow exchange rules
- One **point** of price movement has a fixed dollar value
- You trade **contracts**, not lots

## What is a mini?

**Mini** is the standard E-mini (or equivalent) size — `NQ`, `ES`, `YM`, `GC`, `CL`. One mini NQ is **$20 per index point**. A 10-point move is **$200** per contract.

Historically “E-mini” was already smaller than the original full-size S&P. In Hextrade, mini = the main listed contract.

## What is a micro?

A **micro** is usually **1/10 of the mini**. Start with 1 micro when you attach an algorithm or test a webhook.

<ContractSpecsTable group="futures" />

<Warning>
  One mini is about 10× the dollar risk of one micro (except Nikkei, which is 1/5). Do not flip an account from micro to mini without changing quantity.
</Warning>

## Futures P&L

```
P&L = price move × contracts × $/point
```

Example — NQ up 10 points:

- 1 **MNQ**: `10 × $2 = $20`
- 1 **NQ**: `10 × $20 = $200`
- 3 MNQ: `10 × 3 × $2 = $60`

## Ticks vs points

A **tick** is the smallest price increment. A **point** is 1.00 on the quote.

| Ticker | Tick | Mini $ / tick | Micro $ / tick |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| NQ / MNQ | 0.25 | $5 | $0.50 |
| ES / MES | 0.25 | $12.50 | $1.25 |
| YM / MYM | 1.00 | $5 | $0.50 |
| GC / MGC | 0.10 | $10 | $1 |
| CL / MCL | 0.01 | $10 | $1 |
| NKD / MKD | 5.00 | $25 | $5 |

Webhook `tp` and `sl` are **ticks**, not dollars. 20 ticks on NQ = 5 points = $10 on MNQ.

## What to set on a futures account

Choose **mini** or **micro** so it matches the symbol the account actually trades. Confirm one small fill before you scale.
