Low-latency futures. Algorithms, webhooks, and copy trade. Webhook `platformType`: `rithmic`.

## How to connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Add account">
    [Accounts](/accounts) → **Add account** → Rithmic. Premium is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the server / system name">
    Must match what the FCM or prop issued. Examples: `Rithmic 01`, `Rithmic Paper Trading`, `Apex`, `TopstepTrader`, `LucidTrading`, `Earn2Trade`, `Bulenox`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Username and password">
    R | Trader / firm credentials. Verify, then select the account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Wrong system name is the usual fail. Copy it from the firm’s welcome email or R | Trader login screen.
</Warning>

## How to sign contracts

1. Complete FCM or prop onboarding.
2. In **R | Trader** (or the firm portal), accept **market-data agreements** for the exchanges you trade (CME, CBOT, COMEX, NYMEX).
3. Enable the products you need. Unsigned books will reject.
4. Then connect those credentials in Hextrade.
