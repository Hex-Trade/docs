Send the symbol the **connected account** trades. A futures Tradovate account wants `NQ`. A TradeLocker CFD account wants `NAS100`. Mixing them is the most common silent reject.

## Futures

Use CME-style roots: `ES`, `NQ`, `YM`, `RTY`, `GC`, `CL`, `MES`, `MNQ`, `MGC`. Match the contract the broker lists on that account.

## CFDs

Use the broker’s CFD name: `NAS100`, `US500`, `GER40`. See [CFDs](/cfds).

## Hyperliquid

`NAS100` remaps to `xyz:XYZ100` on Hyperliquid. Details: [Crypto](/crypto).

## TradingView chart vs payload

The chart symbol can differ from `ticker` in the JSON. Use `symbolOverride` on the webhook if you need the chart on a continuous contract but the broker wants a specific root.
