Forex and CFDs. Algorithms yes. Webhook and copy-trade coverage is limited (beta). Webhook `platformType`: `ctrader`.

## How to connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Add account">
    [Accounts](/accounts) → **Add account** → cTrader. Premium is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the cTrader login">
    Use the broker’s cTrader credentials. Coverage is still expanding.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to sign contracts

Sign the broker’s cTrader / client agreement in their portal first.
