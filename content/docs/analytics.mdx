There are two places to read performance: the **Analytics** page for everything combined, and **Account stats** on a single row.

## General analytics

Open [dash.hextrade.io/analytics](https://dash.hextrade.io/analytics).

- Combined P&L
- Trade volume
- Win rate
- Performance over daily / weekly / monthly ranges
- Portfolio-level drawdown and other risk figures

Use date filters when you want a tighter window. This page is live account performance, not the Portfolio Builder **Analytics** tab.

## Account-specific analytics

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Accounts">
    [dash.hextrade.io/accounts](https://dash.hextrade.io/accounts)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the account">
    Click the eye icon or the account name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the detail">
    Balance and buying power, open and closed positions, trade history, P&L by day / week / month, algorithm performance, and max drawdown.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Metrics

| Metric | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **P&L** | Gains minus losses for the period |
| **Win rate** | Share of trades that closed in profit |
| **Drawdown** | Largest peak-to-trough decline |
| **Total trades** | Sample size — more trades, more reliable stats |
| **Avg. trade duration** | How long positions stay open |
| **Sharpe ratio** | Risk-adjusted return |

<Tip>
  Watch drawdown closely on funded prop accounts. Most firms have a max-drawdown rule. Compare algorithms across brokers, then pair losing days with the [Calendar](/calendar).
</Tip>
