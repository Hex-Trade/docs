<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is the difference between a mini and a micro?">
    A micro is usually **1/10** of the mini’s dollar-per-point (Nikkei is 1/5). 1 NQ = $20/pt; 1 MNQ = $2/pt. Full table: [Futures](/futures).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How is futures P&L calculated?">
    `price move × contracts × $/point`. Examples are on [Futures](/futures).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I match 1 MNQ on a CFD broker?">
    `lots = futures $/pt ÷ CFD pt/lot`. With a $1 NAS100 pt/lot, **2.00 lots ≈ 1 MNQ**. Confirm the broker spec on [CFDs](/cfds).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is nano?">
    A very small CFD / forex lot (often 0.001). Hextrade has no nano contract type — use CFD mode and a small lot.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I size XYZ100 like 1 NQ?">
    No. Hyperliquid uses `sz` in USDC P&L space. Confirm min size on that market. [Crypto](/crypto).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Should I raise master size or the follower multiplier?">
    Keep the master conservative. Scale each follower with its **risk multiplier**. A 2.0× funded follower can hit daily loss while the master is fine.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
