Multi-asset. Algorithms and webhooks. No copy trade. Webhook `platformType`: `mt5`. MetaTrader 4 is not connectable yet.

## How to connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Add account">
    [Accounts](/accounts) → **Add account** → MetaTrader 5. Premium is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Login, password, server">
    Investor passwords cannot trade. Use the master password. Pick the broker server from the list or enter a custom server. Verify and add.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to sign contracts

Open the account at the broker or prop, accept their client agreement, and wait for the MT5 login email. Then connect here.
