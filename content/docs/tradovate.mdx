Futures. Algorithms, webhooks, and copy trade. Webhook `platformType`: `tradovate`.

## How to connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Add account">
    [Accounts](/accounts) → **Add account** → Tradovate. Premium is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick Demo or Live">
    Demo is the practice environment. Live is a funded or personal Tradovate account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter username and password">
    The same login you use on trader.tradovate.com. Device ID can stay blank.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept Tradovate terms">
    Check the terms box. Hextrade submits the agreement when you verify.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify, then select accounts">
    Choose one or more Tradovate accounts from the list and add them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can generate a Tradovate trial demo from **Tools** on Accounts, then add it here.
</Tip>

## How to sign contracts

1. Create or log in at [trader.tradovate.com](https://trader.tradovate.com).
2. Accept **exchange agreements** (CME, CBOT, NYMEX, COMEX) and market-data subscriptions in Tradovate. Orders reject until those are signed.
3. In Hextrade, accept [Tradovate Terms](https://www.tradovate.com/terms/) on the Add account form. Hextrade calls Tradovate `agree-terms` when you verify.
4. Prop accounts: also sign the **firm’s** evaluation or funded agreement on the firm site first. Then use those Tradovate credentials here.
