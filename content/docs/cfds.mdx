A **contract for difference** is an OTC product from your broker. You do not own the future. You trade the price difference.

- Lot size and **$/point** are set by the broker, not CME
- Names look like `NAS100`, `USTEC`, `US100`, `XAUUSD` — not `NQ`
- 1 lot on one broker is often not 1 lot on another
- You trade **lots**, then set **pt/lot** in Hextrade

Exchange futures sizing (mini / micro / ticks) is on [Futures](/futures). Hyperliquid tokenized indexes are on [Crypto](/crypto).

## What is a nano?

**Nano** is a CFD / forex size, not a CME futures contract. There is no `NNQ` in Hextrade.

- **Forex** — 0.001 lot (100 units). Mini lot = 0.1, micro lot = 0.01, standard = 1.0
- **Index CFDs** — a very small broker-defined size, sometimes 0.01 lot

Use **MT5 / CFD** mode and a small lot, or a lower pt/lot, to approximate nano risk.

## What is pt/lot?

**pt/lot** is dollars of PnL for **1.00 price point** on **1.00 lot**.

```
P&L = price move × lots × pt/lot
```

<ContractSpecsTable group="cfd" />

Hextrade Portfolio defaults when pt/lot is empty:

- Index CFDs (`NAS100`, `US500`, `US30`, `JPN225`) → **$1 / point / lot**
- Gold (`XAUUSD`) → **$100 / point / lot** (100 oz)
- Oil (`USOIL`) → **$100 / point / lot** (100 bbl)

Override if your broker’s spec is different.

## CFD P&L examples

`NAS100` at the default $1/pt/lot, up 10 points:

- 1.00 lot: `10 × $1 = $10`
- 2.00 lots: `10 × $2 = $20` ← same as **1 MNQ**
- 20.00 lots: `10 × $20 = $200` ← same as **1 NQ**

## Match a futures micro on a CFD

```
lots = futures $/pt  ÷  your CFD pt/lot
```

| You want | Futures $/pt | CFD pt/lot | Lots |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 MNQ | $2 | $1 | **2.00** |
| 1 NQ | $20 | $1 | **20.00** |
| 1 MES | $5 | $1 | **5.00** |
| 1 MGC | $10 | $100 | **0.10** |
| 1 MCL | $100 | $100 | **1.00** |

<Tip>
  Gold and oil CFDs are often $100/pt per lot. 0.10 lot of XAUUSD ≈ 1 MGC, not 1 GC.
</Tip>

## What to set on a CFD account

Set contract type to `mt5`, pick the broker ticker (`NAS100`, `XAUUSD`, …), then set **pt/lot** from the spec sheet. Confirm one small fill before you scale.
