Open **Trade** in the sidebar. The page goes full-screen so the chart and ticket have room. Use any [connected broker](/brokers).

<AllPlatformPills />

## What you’ll see

- **Chart** — TradingView-style. Change symbol, timeframe, and chart type.
- **Order panel** — Market, limit, or stop. Buy or sell.
- **Positions / orders** — Open positions and working orders when the broker provides them.
- **Account selector** — Pick which connected account to trade.

## Place an order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select symbol and account">
    Confirm you are on the account you intend to trade.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose type and side">
    Market, limit, or stop. Buy or sell.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter size and price">
    Quantity always. Limit / stop also need price (and stop price if required).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Confirm if the ticket shows a summary. Fields depend on the broker.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Chart settings

Open **Chart settings** from the sidebar or the gear on the Trade page.

### Appearance

- Theme — Dark, Light, TradingView-style, Professional
- Background color
- Chart style — Candlestick, Line, Area, Bars

### Colors

Bull / bear candles, wicks, grid, and labels.

### Chart elements

Grid, volume, crosshair, legend, line width.

### Text

Font size (typically 8–20) and family (Inter, Arial, Monospace).

## Options

If the account and broker support it, **Options** in the sidebar can place single-leg or multi-leg strategies.
