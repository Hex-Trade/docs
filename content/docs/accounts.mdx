Open **Accounts** from the sidebar. This is the first screen after login and the place you add, inspect, and remove broker connections.

<Video src="/videos/Add-Account.webm" />

## Summary cards

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Portfolio value** | Total value across connected accounts. Updates with live P&L when available. |
| **Completed trades** | Closed trades across accounts. |
| **Active accounts** | How many brokers you have connected. |
| **Account limit** | Your plan’s maximum. Raise it in [Subscriptions](/subscriptions). |

The table under the cards lists each account’s name, platform, status, and P&L, plus actions such as **Account stats** and disconnect.

## Add an account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Accounts">
    Go to [dash.hextrade.io/accounts](https://dash.hextrade.io/accounts).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add account">
    Choose the broker. Limits come from your [subscription](/subscriptions).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the connection">
    Sign in or paste API keys for that platform. The row appears in the table when it succeeds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Account stats">
    Review balance, trade history, and broker-specific metrics for that account only.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  On Tradovate you can generate a trial demo from **Tools** on Accounts, then add it to Hextrade.
</Tip>

Connection fields, copy-trade coverage, and webhook `platformType` values live on [Brokers](/brokers).

## What you can do from here

- View every connected account in one table
- Open **Account stats** for a single account
- Use shared reports across accounts
- Open the **Webhooks** section to copy your unique URL
- Add more brokers until you hit the plan limit

## Limits

When you hit the account cap, **Add account** shows a message. Upgrade in **Subscriptions**. Each broker connection counts as one slot. “Unlimited” plans show no cap on this page.
