Futures. Shares the Tradovate backend. Algorithms, webhooks, and copy trade. Webhook `platformType`: `ninjatrader`.

## How to connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Add account">
    [Accounts](/accounts) → **Add account** → NinjaTrader. Premium is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick Demo or Live">
    Match the environment on your NinjaTrader / Tradovate login.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Username and password">
    The NinjaTrader account that is linked to Tradovate execution.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept terms">
    Same Tradovate terms checkbox as the Tradovate flow. Verify, then pick accounts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to sign contracts

1. Finish NinjaTrader account setup and link Tradovate if the firm requires it.
2. Sign CME / market-data agreements in Tradovate (or the firm’s portal).
3. Accept Tradovate terms in Hextrade when you connect.
4. Prop users: sign the firm contract first, then connect.
