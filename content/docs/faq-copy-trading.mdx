<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How does copy trading work?">
    One **master** account’s fills are replicated to **follower** accounts in the same group. Open [dash.hextrade.io/copy-trade-groups](https://dash.hextrade.io/copy-trade-groups). Setup: [Copy trading](/copy-trading).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which platforms can be master or follower?">
    ProjectX, Tradovate, NinjaTrader, Rithmic, and Volumetrica. Mixed groups among those five are supported.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does the risk multiplier do?">
    It scales follower quantity vs the master. `1.0` = same size, `0.5` = half, `2.0` = double. Set it per follower against that account’s capital and prop rules.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will deleting a group close follower positions?">
    No. Deleting a group stops future copies. Existing follower positions are unchanged. Deactivate a group if you only want to pause copying.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can’t I create another group?">
    Copy-trade group count can be capped by your [subscription](/subscriptions). Upgrade if the dashboard reports a group limit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I copy from Tradovate to TradeLocker or MT5?">
    Not through copy-trade groups. Those platforms run algorithms and webhooks but are not copy master/follower.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
