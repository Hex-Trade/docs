---
title: Copy trading
description: One master account, many followers — each with its own risk multiplier.
---

Open [dash.hextrade.io/copy-trade-groups](https://dash.hextrade.io/copy-trade-groups). Trades on the **master** replicate to every **follower** in the group.

Copy trading works on **ProjectX**, **Tradovate**, **NinjaTrader**, **Rithmic**, and **Volumetrica**. Cross-platform groups among those five are supported. Other brokers can run algorithms and webhooks but cannot be master or follower.

## What you get

<Cards>
  <Card title="Risk multipliers">
    `1.0` matches the master. `0.5` is half size. `2.0` is double. Set this per follower.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Named groups">
    Give each group a name and a description so you remember the strategy later.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Instant copy">
    Master fills replicate automatically. Deactivate a group to pause without deleting it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Group stats">
    Success rate and P&L sit on the groups table next to master and follower count.
  </Card>
</Cards>

## Create a group

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add Group">
    Open Copy Trade Groups and click **Add Group**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Use something you will recognize later, such as `NQ Scalp — Low Risk`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the master">
    This should be the account already running your main algorithm or strategy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add followers">
    Accounts must already be connected. See [Accounts](/accounts).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set multipliers">
    Match each follower’s capital and prop-firm rules. A high multiplier on a funded account can breach limits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and activate">
    Set status to **Active** to start copying.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The Copy Trade page

Summary cards at the top:

- Total groups
- Active groups
- Total followers
- Success rate

The table lists master, follower count, status, success rate, and total P&L.

## Managing groups

| Action | Result |
| --- | --- |
| **View** | Master, followers, history, and stats |
| **Activate / Deactivate** | Pause copying without deleting |
| **Edit** | Name, description, master, followers, multiplier |
| **Delete** | Removes the group. Followers stay connected. |

<Tip>
  Run `0.5x` on the most sensitive funded account and `1.0x` on a personal account. Watch group performance in [Analytics](/analytics).
</Tip>
