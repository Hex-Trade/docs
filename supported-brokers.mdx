## What “supported” means

- **Connect** — Link your broker account from the dashboard (login, API keys, or the platform’s connection flow).
- **See in one place** — Connected accounts appear on [Accounts](/accounts) with portfolio value, trades, and status.
- **Trade** — Use the [Trade](/trading) page to place orders on the selected account (order types depend on the broker).
- **Algorithms** — Run Hextrade algorithms on connected futures-capable accounts. Oanda is excluded from futures algorithms.
- **Copy trade** — Use ProjectX, Tradovate, NinjaTrader, Rithmic, or Volumetrica as master or follower in [Copy Trading](/copy-trading/setup).
- **Webhooks** — Drive orders from TradingView on webhook-enabled platforms. See [TradingView webhooks](/external-algorithms/tradingview-webhooks).

## Connecting an account

1. Open **[Accounts](/accounts)** from the sidebar.
2. Click **Add account** and choose the broker.
3. Complete the platform’s sign-in or API key flow.
4. After connection, the account appears in the Accounts table with its name, platform, and status.
5. Open **Account stats** for that account to see performance and history.

<Tip>
For Tradovate, you can generate a trial demo from **Tools** on the Accounts page, then add it to Hextrade if you want.
</Tip>

Account limits depend on your [subscription](/subscriptions). If you hit the limit, you’ll see a message; upgrade your plan to add more accounts.

## Broker-specific details

### ProjectX

Prop-firm futures. Connect with username, password, and server. Supports algorithms, TradingView webhooks, and copy trading.

### Tradovate / NinjaTrader

Futures. Connect with username, password, and environment (demo or live). NinjaTrader uses the same backend as Tradovate. Both support algorithms, webhooks, and copy trading.

### Rithmic

Low-latency futures. Connect with server, username, and password, then select the account. Supports algorithms, webhooks, and copy trading.

### TradeLocker / MatchTrader / Plus500 / MetaTrader 5

Multi-asset and prop-firm platforms. Connect via each platform’s login flow. Algorithms and webhooks are supported; copy trading is not.

### Oanda

Forex and CFDs. Connect and trade currency pairs. Oanda accounts are not offered on futures algorithms.

### Hyperliquid

Crypto perps. Connect a Hyperliquid wallet/account. Webhook `platformType` is `hyperliquid`.

### CQG / Volumetrica / cTrader

Volumetrica supports copy trading and webhooks. CQG can be connected for account management and algorithms. **cTrader** is in beta; webhook and copy-trade coverage is more limited.
