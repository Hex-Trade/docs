## Profile Information

Keep your account details accurate so notifications, support, and integrations work correctly.

<video src="/videos/Account-details1.mp4" controls />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Open **Settings** from the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit your details">
    Update **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Phone Number**, **TradingView Username**, and **Discord Username**. Email is read-only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Changes">
    Click **Save Changes**. Updates apply immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **First Name / Last Name** | Used across the platform and in notifications |
| **Email Address** | Read-only — set at registration |
| **Phone Number** | Include country code, e.g. `+1 555 000 0000` |
| **TradingView Username** | Connect your TradingView account to claim exclusive indicators — Premium and Ultimate only |
| **Discord Username** | Enter without the `@`. Links your Hex dashboard to Discord so Hex AI can see your accounts and algos when you chat. You must be in the HexTrade Discord server for the link to work. Also required for Discord DM alerts. |

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

2FA protects your account with a time-based one-time code in addition to your password.

<video src="/videos/placeholder.mp4" controls />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Enable 2FA">
    The setup flow begins and a QR code is displayed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the QR code">
    Open **Google Authenticator**, **Authy**, **1Password**, or any TOTP app → tap **+** → **Scan a QR code**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the 6-digit code">
    Type the code shown in your authenticator app and click **Verify**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your backup codes">
    Store the one-time backup codes somewhere safe and offline. Each code can only be used once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Disable 2FA:** Click **Disable 2FA** and confirm with a live authenticator code.

<Warning>
  Enable 2FA before connecting live funded accounts. If you lose access to your authenticator app without backup codes, account recovery will require identity verification.
</Warning>

## Notifications

Configure exactly where and when Hextrade sends you alerts.

<video src="/videos/placeholder.mp4" controls />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the master switch">
    **All Notifications** silences every alert when disabled, without changing individual settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose delivery channels">
    Enable **Discord Webhook**, **Discord DMs**, and/or **Email Alerts**. Discord DMs require a Discord username in Profile. Use **Test** to confirm a webhook URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose alert types">
    Turn on the events you want: Trade Opened, Trade Closed, Trade Error, Connection Error, Account Updated, and System Alert.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set sound alerts">
    Toggle browser audio on every execution and adjust **Volume** (0–100%).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Notification Settings">
    Click **Save Notification Settings** to apply all changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## API Token

Generate a key to authenticate against the HexTrade API and access your trade feed from external tools or scripts.

<video src="/videos/placeholder.mp4" controls />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Expand API Token">
    Open the **API Token** section on Settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate Key">
    Click **Generate Key**. Copy the key immediately — it is shown only once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Store the key">
    Save it in a secret manager or environment variable. Use it on `wss://api.hextrade.io/alerts?apiKey=hxt_...` or `GET api.hextrade.io/user-webhooks/trades`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Rotate or revoke:** Click **Regenerate Key** or **Revoke Key** at any time. Regenerating or revoking immediately invalidates the previous key.

The in-page **Setup Guide** covers WebSocket frames (`trade`, `algoTrade`, subscribe/unsubscribe), keepalive pings, and REST history filters. The dashboard **Feed** page is a real-time monitor with stats by algo, open positions, and history.

<Warning>
  Never commit your API token to a public repository or share it in Discord. Treat it like a password. If it is compromised, generate a new token immediately.
</Warning>
