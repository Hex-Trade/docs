Four steps to start using Hextrade. Each step includes a walkthrough video.

---

## 1. Create your account

Register at [dash.hextrade.io/signup](https://dash.hextrade.io/signup) with email or Google.

<video src="/videos/Create-Account.mp4" controls />

Need more detail? [Account creation](/get-started/account-creation)

---

## 2. Log in to your account

Log in at [dash.hextrade.io/login](https://dash.hextrade.io/login). After sign-in you land on **Accounts**.

<video src="/videos/Login-Account.mp4" controls />

Need more detail? [Account login](/get-started/account-login)

---

## 3. Purchase premium (optional)

Free accounts can connect brokers and explore the dashboard. Premium unlocks paid algorithms and higher limits.

Purchase at [dash.hextrade.io/subscriptions](https://dash.hextrade.io/subscriptions).

<video src="/videos/Purchase-Sub.mp4" controls />

Need more detail? [Subscriptions](/subscriptions)

---

## 4. Connect a broker account

Go to [Accounts](https://dash.hextrade.io/accounts) and add your first broker or prop firm account. See [Supported Brokers](/supported-brokers) for the full list.

<video src="/videos/Add-Account.webm" controls style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "0.5rem" }} />

Need more detail? [Accounts](/accounts)

---
