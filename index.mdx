# Welcome To Hextrade

Welcome to the **Hextrade** documentation. This guide covers everything you need to use the platform — from creating your account and connecting a broker, to running algorithms and setting up copy trading.

Hextrade is a **copy-trade platform** that blends automation with AI. You can use our built-in algorithms, copy trades across multiple accounts with different risk profiles, or connect your own strategy via a **TradingView webhook**.

<Warning>
  Trading involves significant risk of loss. The content in these docs is for informational purposes only and does not constitute financial advice. Always do your own research and never risk more than you can afford to lose.
</Warning>

## Getting Started

Walk through the four steps — each one has a video — on [Getting Started](/getting-started).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    [Register at dash.hextrade.io/signup](https://dash.hextrade.io/signup)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in to your account">
    [Log in at dash.hextrade.io/login](https://dash.hextrade.io/login)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Purchase premium (optional)">
    [Purchase at dash.hextrade.io/subscriptions](https://dash.hextrade.io/subscriptions)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect a broker account">
    Go to [Accounts](https://dash.hextrade.io/accounts) and add your first broker or prop firm account. See [Supported Brokers](/supported-brokers) for the full list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Supported Brokers & Platforms

| Platform | Type | Status |
|----------|------|--------|
| **ProjectX** | Prop firm / Futures | Available |
| **Tradovate** | Futures | Available |
| **NinjaTrader** | Futures | Available |
| **Rithmic** | Futures | Available |
| **TradeLocker** | Multi-asset / Prop | Available |
| **Plus500** | CFDs / Multi-asset | Available |
| **Oanda** | Forex & CFDs | Available |
| **Hyperliquid** | Crypto perps | Available |
| **CQG** | Futures & Derivatives | Available |
| **MatchTrader** | Prop firm / Forex & CFDs | Available |
| **Volumetrica** | Futures / Order-flow | Available |
| **cTrader** | Forex & CFDs | Beta |
| **MetaTrader 5** | Multi-asset | Available |

Copy trading is available on **ProjectX**, **Tradovate**, **NinjaTrader**, **Rithmic**, and **Volumetrica**. See [Supported Brokers](/supported-brokers) for connection details.
