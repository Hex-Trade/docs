Strike is a gold futures algorithm. It is **deprecated** in the current catalog — existing connections still work, but prefer a newer GC family such as [Pow](/algorithms/pow) or [Vix](/algorithms/vix).

---

---

## Connecting Strike

1. Go to [dash.hextrade.io/algos](https://dash.hextrade.io/algos) and find `GCSTRIKE`.
2. Review performance metrics before connecting.
3. Link your broker account via **Connected Accounts**.
4. Set risk and activate.

<Warning>
  5-minute strategies generate frequent signals and can accumulate commissions quickly. Ensure your account has sufficient margin and review commission costs with your broker.
</Warning>
