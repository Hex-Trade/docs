Spark is an intraday strategy available on Nasdaq 100 (NQ) and Gold (GC).

---

| Dashboard ID | Instrument | Notes |
|--------------|-----------|-------|
| `NQSPARK` | Nasdaq 100 (NQ) | Prop firm ready |
| `NQSPARKV2` | Nasdaq 100 (NQ) | Revision 2 |
| `GCSPARK` | Gold (GC) | |

---

## Connecting Spark

1. Go to [dash.hextrade.io/algos](https://dash.hextrade.io/algos) and find `NQSPARK`, `NQSPARKV2`, or `GCSPARK`.
2. Review performance metrics on the detail page.
3. Connect your broker account via **Connected Accounts**.
4. Set risk level and activate.

<Warning>
  Trading involves risk of loss. Always verify performance metrics and use appropriate position sizing before going live.
</Warning>
