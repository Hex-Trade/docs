Pulse is a 5-minute strategy running on Nasdaq 100 futures (NQ).

---

## Available Variants

| Dashboard ID | Instrument | Timeframe |
|--------------|-----------|----------|
| `NQPULSE` | Nasdaq 100 (NQ) | Intraday |

---

## Connecting Pulse

1. Go to [dash.hextrade.io/algos](https://dash.hextrade.io/algos) and find `NQPULSE`.
2. Review performance metrics on the detail page.
3. Connect your account, set risk, and activate.

<Warning>
  5-minute strategies generate frequent signals. Monitor your account closely when first activating and ensure your broker supports the trade volume.
</Warning>
