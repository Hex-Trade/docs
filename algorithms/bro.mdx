# 😎 Bro

Bro is a 15-minute strategy running on S&P 500 futures (ES).

---

## Available Variants

| Dashboard ID | Instrument | Timeframe |
|--------------|-----------|----------|
| `ESBRO` | S&P 500 (ES) | Intraday |

---

## Connecting Bro

1. Go to [dash.hextrade.io/algos](https://dash.hextrade.io/algos) and find `ESBRO`.
2. Review metrics before connecting your account.
3. Connect via **Connected Accounts**, set risk, and activate.

<Warning>
  Trading involves risk of loss. Review performance history carefully and use appropriate position sizing.
</Warning>
